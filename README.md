**VIVET SPEEDRUN: Run against any player in the world, find the item first, and win.**

Add friends, 1v1s, host custom lobbies of up to 16, or even play solo. Build your block collection, rise on the leaderboards, or find all the custom achievements.

![A full solo match](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/fSGdg6ER/images/3f535202813e9abc430f28bede652784d9e0e8a0.gif)

## Difficulties

I host a variety of difficulties, including:

- **QUICK:** Lightning fast games, most under a minute, with a custom selection algorithm based on where you spawn.
- **MEDIUM:** Mining or loot based rounds, testing your planning and preparation skills.
- **HARD:** Similar to Medium, but items are harder to get.
- **EXTREME:** For hardcore players who know their way around the game.
- **IMPOSSIBLE:** Unfair items that require both luck and skill to obtain. For those who love pain!

The higher the difficulty, the more rewards you gain. Experience, Elo, coins. XP is scaled based on estimated playtime, so if x number of Quick games take as long as an average Impossible game, you will ultimately end up with the same amount of XP.

## Solo Mode

If you can't find yourself a competitive match, or simply want to play alone, you can run for blocks with only the timer against you. Solo completions count for item leaderboards, allowing you to climb the ranks and obtain items faster than any player before. Every solo completion also counts towards your collection and level, on top of coins.

![Solo match in progress](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/fSGdg6ER/images/88953c8ce0679aaf39b55a45a9f4505292f255ae.png)

## Ranked Mode

Run 1v1s against opponents across the world for Elo. Whoever gets the item in the lowest amount of time wins. Depending on your Elo, you are placed into a rank:

**Coal, Copper, Iron, Gold, Emerald, Diamond, Netherite, or Grand Champion.**

When you play for the first time, your rank will be determined with 5 placement games. The harder the match, the more points. Once reaching Grand Champion, you will be running for leaderboard spots. Each month the leaderboard spots reset, granting trophies to victors. All Grand Champion ranks get set back to their baseline values, so you can instantly start running for leaderboard spots after a reset. The Trophies for the leaderboard are as follows:

- **Speed King** (Top 1)
- **Speed Prince** (Top 5)
- **Speed Knight** (Top 20)
- **Speed Squire** (Top 50)

![Image shows a user with Speed King](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/fSGdg6ER/images/4cc14458a452e6ea8c57f769be3411a155976ebf.png)

## Leaderboards

There are a plethora of different leaderboards, all free for you to compete on!

Ladder (Elo), Most XP, Total Wins, Win Streak, Best Streak, Fastest Times, Playtime, and Monthly Seed.

Ladder and Monthly Seed are the only boards to reset each month. On the Ladder, only Grand Champions get reset. Fastest Times includes all items and the top times among players, separated by difficulty tiers.

## Friends

You are able to add players as friends, and run against them. Play in unranked with no Elo on the line, or private matches where you can invite up to 16 players. Private matches can be joined through a code, and can be joined by anyone. If you want to, you can also set them to be friends only.

Private matches feature a variety of modifiers, which can either make the game stupid easy or ridiculously hard.

For any player you have added, you can view them in your friends list. This is where you send invites, chats, and view profiles. You can also receive notifications about friends in your inbox.

## Other Modes

While the main modes are the general way to play, there are a handful of special modes you can also try!

- **Practice:** Pick any item from your Collection and practice running it. You can choose a custom seed or set to random.
- **Nether Only:** Spawn in the Nether and run for a custom preset of blocks. As of now, this mode is only in private lobbies.
- **Monthly Seed:** I choose a seed every month, and you can run to be the fastest for that specific seed! Each difficulty tier has a different item, with its own leaderboard.

In the future, I plan to add 2v2s and Nether Only for other modes.

## Collection

Your Collection tracks every item you've run, giving you a statistic of how many unique items you've obtained. View your time, how many attempts you've had on it, how many times you've obtained it, or practice a run for that item specifically.

If you obtain an item for the first time, you get rewarded with a coin bonus. In Solo, a "Pity" system weighs the items you haven't found and makes them more common to roll. It's on by default, you can turn it off in settings, and it only works in Solo.

## Progression

As you play, you earn XP. For each level you gain, there are special rewards.

- **Level Road:** From levels 1 to 100, you earn increasingly more coins. There are also special cosmetics you can unlock along the way. After level 100, you can still level up, but it becomes very difficult.
- **Vivet Coins:** Through Daily Quests, New Items in Collection, Achievements, Level-Ups, and Monthly Seed completions, you can earn Vivet Coins. These coins allow you to buy my custom (and self-admitted), COOL AND EPIC cosmetics.
- **Daily Quests:** Every day, you are given 3 quests to complete. Each quest rolls a rarity, with the hardest being the most rare.
- **Achievements:** 100+ custom and (WELL TESTED) achievements you can obtain, ranging from easy to pretty hard. If that's not enough, you can hunt for my special **SECRET ACHIEVEMENTS**. These hidden suckers range from mind-numbingly easy but incredibly random, to HOW WAS I SUPPOSED TO THINK OF THIS? Impossibly hard. Legally, I do not encourage you to seek all of these out. Many actively require you to ignore the entire point of the game to do something completely pointless.
- **Shop and Locker:** Use your hard earned Vivet Coins in the shop, where you can buy cosmetics. Emblems, Backgrounds, Titles, Intros and Outros. All of them can be seen by the player you run against. Outros are a special variant that plays for your opponent if you beat them.
- **Rarities:** All items/systems based on rarities follow these 5: Common, Rare, Epic, Astral, and Secret. Astral is the most prestigious, with Secret being either unfair or incredibly random.

![Image shows end of match rewards](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/fSGdg6ER/images/9ec16a3347f75478a71288a41c049fbbf7f45123.png)

Important to note, **EVERYTHING IN GAME can be obtained through playtime. No cosmetics or otherwise are sold for real life money.**
